FAQ

Common questions

Can PNGee build websites as well as IT infrastructure?

Yes. PNGee can build business websites, redesign existing sites and support the surrounding domain, DNS, Cloudflare, email and hosting foundations.

Do you build custom web applications and SaaS MVPs?

Yes. PNGee can help with discovery, design and development for custom web applications, portals, dashboards and SaaS MVPs. The offer is custom project delivery, not a catalogue of finished subscription SaaS products.

Can you set up Cloudflare, domains and business email?

Yes. PNGee can help connect domains, configure Cloudflare DNS, set up branded email routing and plan SPF, DKIM and DMARC records so the business identity is cleaner and safer.

Do you only work with large businesses?

No. PNGee is positioned to help small and medium businesses that need practical website, cloud application, networking and cybersecurity support without maintaining a full internal technical team.

What happens during the Network & Security Health Check?

The assessment reviews relevant physical, wireless, routing, firewall and segmentation aspects of the environment. Findings are translated into prioritised remediation recommendations.

Can you help with an existing network instead of replacing everything?

Yes. The preferred approach is to identify what is actually causing the problem before recommending replacement. Existing equipment can remain where it is fit for purpose.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Recurring network and security support can be scoped around the size of the environment, the systems under management and the required response model.
