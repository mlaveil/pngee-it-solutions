FAQ

Common questions

Do you only work with large businesses?

No. PNGee is specifically positioned to help small and medium businesses that need competent networking and cybersecurity support without maintaining a full internal infrastructure team.

What happens during the Network & Security Health Check?

The assessment reviews relevant physical, wireless, routing, firewall and segmentation aspects of the environment. Findings are translated into prioritised remediation recommendations.

Can you help with an existing network instead of replacing everything?

Yes. The preferred approach is to identify what is actually causing the problem before recommending replacement. Existing equipment can remain where it is fit for purpose.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Recurring network and security support can be scoped around the size of the environment, the systems under management and the required response model.
